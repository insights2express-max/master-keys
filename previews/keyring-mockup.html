What you see

Lobby → KeyRing Setup → game screen → Ring cleared

  1. 1Lobby card, built like the others (collapsed by default; NEW badge for a while), placed before "More games". Details: Rings played, Cleared %, Bonus words; 🆕 New Game (last settings) and ⚙ Setup.
  2. 2Letters on the Ring: 3 to 8, the Player's pick. The row of tiles under it shows the size.
  3. 3Challenge Level (which words go in the slots) and the Timer: Untimed, or 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes per Ring.
  4. 4The slots, shortest first, in LockWord's dark glass panel. Short words share a row when it saves space. Tap a slot to pick it for 💡 Hint (it glows blue).
  5. 5Message strip: the word you are spelling, then what happened (green = slot filled, gold = already found, red = not a word, blue = a clue).
  6. 6The Ring: a 3D glass platform with a steel key ring; the letters are tiles hung on the ring. Swipe (touch) or drag (mouse) across them; go back one tile to undo it; let go to submit. Or tap letters one by one and tap the last one again to submit. Or type (keys shown under the Ring on iPad and desktop).
  7. 7Bonus tag (our own design): a key-fob tag that counts this Ring's bonus words, real words that are not in a slot. Each new one pays 🪙1, and the tag swings. Tap it for the list.
  8. 8Shuffle is the steel hub in the middle of the Ring: tap it and the letters spin to new places (free).
  9. 9💡 Hint, 🪙3 (two taps, as in the other games): shows the picked slot's clue (or the shortest empty slot's). A signal, not a solve: no letters are given. The slot keeps a 💡 mark; tap it to see the clue again, free.
  10. 10Clock: counts down when timed (red in the last 10 seconds); a stopwatch when untimed.
  11. 11🔑 Key Drop, 🪙10 (the "solve" helper): drops 3 random letters into empty slot squares, in light blue so you can tell them from your own. A slot it completes counts as filled.
  12. 12Ring cleared window: your words and bonus words, the coins (🪙5 for the Ring + 🪙1 per bonus word, minus what you spent), then ☰ Lobby · ⚙ Setup · ▶ Next Ring. When the timer runs out, a "Time's up" window shows the missed words in red instead.

Try this

Start a 6-letter Ring (letters of GARDEN). Find DEAR, then GRAD (a bonus word: the tag swings and coins go up). Try XYZ by typing to see "not a word". Use 💡 and 🔑 Drop. Jump to Ring cleared for the end window.

Not in this mockup: sounds, Game History, saving an unfinished Ring, and the level builder (those come with v11). The slot words here come from the Lexicon (levels 1–2) and the bonus words from ENABLE, as v11 will do.
