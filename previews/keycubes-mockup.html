Master Keys · KeyCubes mockup (v12 plan)

A clickable preview of KeyCubes, the letter-cube word hunt (Game Plan G2, RFI CD-103). The game screen really plays: swipe or drag across touching cubes, tap them one by one (tap the last one again to submit), or type and press Enter. The Bot hunts the same grid; a word you both find scores for nobody. Each grid size has three sample grids rolled from our own cubes; every word is checked against ENABLE and Lexicon words show their clue in the results. Coins, records and the Lobby's other games are made up, and the app itself is unchanged.

What you see

Lobby → KeyCubes Setup → game screen → round results → next round

  1. 1Lobby card, built like the others (NEW badge for a while): Matches, Won %, Best word; 🆕 New Game (last settings) and ⚙ Setup. Our own icon: four 3D letter cubes.
  2. 2Play: ⚔ Duel with the Bot (the main game, JM CD-103: 2.1a) or 🎯 Solo practice (no Bot).
  3. 3Grid: 4×4 or 5×5 (6×6 comes in a later build). The row of cubes under it previews the size.
  4. 4Timer (1, 2, 3 or 4 min; Untimed in Solo only) and Bot level 1–10 (which words the Bot knows and how many it finds). Both are asked in the RFI.
  5. 5More options: Shortest word, Rounds per match, Scoring (switch it to compare the two ways), Button Colors (Steel by default).
  6. 6Duel bar on dark glass: you (points so far, before shared words cancel), the clock with ⏸ Pause (tap the clock too), and the Bot (its word count only: its words stay hidden until the end). A gold "+1" pops when it finds one.
  7. 7Message strip: the word you are tracing (blue tiles), then what happened: green = new word + points, orange = already found, red = not a word / too short / not on the grid. A Hint shows here in blue.
  8. 8The tray: a dark glass platform with a slot for every cube; the cubes are 3D tiles (KeyRing's) with a small letter-point value. A trace joins touching cubes across, down or diagonally, each cube once. Last 10 seconds: the tray flashes yellow (10–6) then red (5–1), with ticks in the app.
  9. 9Coin buttons (Steel): 💡 Hint (two taps) shows the clue and length of a Lexicon word you have not found and stripes its first cube: a signal, not a solve. 🔄 Turn (free) gives the grid a quarter turn, which helps you see new words. 🏁 Done ends a Solo round.
  10. 10Your words, newest first, with points.
  11. 11One-row win report in the gold frame (as in every game): "🙂 YOU WIN! 🏆 86–54", "🤖 The Bot WINS!", or "⚖️ DRAW!". Solo: your score and words.
  12. 12Summary: each side's words, the shared words that scored nothing, the match score so far, and the coins.
  13. 13Tabs: Your words · Bot's words · All words (every word in the grid, found or missed).
  14. 14Word card: a mini grid lights the word's path (blue = first cube), the score breakdown (letter points + length bonus = total, or 0 when both found it), and the clue reveal for Lexicon words (the learning reveal from the Game Plan).
  15. 15☰ Lobby · ⚙ Setup · ▶ Next round (a fresh grid; after the last round, ▶ New match).

Sample scoring (asked in the RFI)

Letter points + length bonus: each letter's value comes from how often it appears in our Lexicon (common = 1, rare = 10). Length bonus:

Our cubes (asked in the RFI)

Six faces each. Every letter gets at least one face; the rest follow our Lexicon's letter counts. Q is always the "Qu" face. 4×4 set (16 cubes):

Try this

Start a 4×4 duel, trace a few words, try a nonsense word by typing, use 💡 Hint and 🔄 Turn, then press ⏩ Last 12 seconds to see the flash, or ⏹ End round now. In the results, tap words to see their path, points and clue. The 5×5 grids have a Qu cube (QUIET, QUIRK, LIQUID…).

Not in this mockup: sounds, the roll animation, Game History, saving an unfinished match, and the Bot's real level tables (those come with v12).
